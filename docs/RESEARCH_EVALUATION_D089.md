# D089: development repairs and availability-based cohort revision

D088 remains preserved: six documented bugs refused by the narrow method
contract; ActiveMQ cannot fill a 1,500-build window. This revision is authorized
by the owner after those feasibility findings. No brake failure counts, policy
predictions or misses have been inspected. Do not relabel D088 as successful.

## Moonshot development

Keep all six development cases and all twelve reserved repositories unchanged.
The diagnosed changes include instance array-return methods, added enum methods,
callbacks/generic objects, logging/file output, date/double/object solar APIs,
and reflection/interpreter state. A single instance-method change does not
support all of these. First support a public no-argument constructor with one
method invocation per fresh JVM, plus bounded integer-array outputs. Report
constructor execution and fresh receiver semantics explicitly. No arbitrary
object fabrication or invocation sequences are assumed.

Verify the published stock-span trigger locally before automatic search. Keep
that oracle input out of comparative search. Bound output size, confirmation,
array shrinking and process lifetimes. Extend the standalone reproducer too.
Do not open the twelve reserved cases to guide engineering. Unsupported cases
and environment problems retain separate statuses.

## Brake cohort revision

Retain ActiveMQ rather than swapping projects. Use only PRs targeting main.
Group all jobs by exact build-head SHA, retain its earliest build and that
build's jobs; record later reruns separately to avoid future outcomes leaking
back into an earlier split. Sort by timestamp then SHA. With at least 200
distinct heads, use 60 percent training, 20 percent audit, remainder evaluation
(integer floors for the first two). A smaller cohort means wider uncertainty,
not permission to lower the 0.05 risk target or existing evidence gates.

This produces a candidate list only. Fetch immutable Git identities and verify
ancestry and branch provenance before eligibility. PRs targeting the same branch
can be siblings; do not count them as ancestor history. Assemble complete job
inventories, preserve raw failures and timeouts, and never substitute filtered
outcomes for the primary study. Rows without complete provenance cannot support
skipping. Keep all train/audit/evaluation identities fixed after creating the
manifest. If ancestry/evidence is insufficient, publish full-suite fallback and
do not describe the cohort as a validated savings study.

Baseline policies, metrics and the measured-versus-estimated cost distinction
remain as in D088. Freeze the final application source/config before reading
evaluation outcomes. No claim of independent CI savings has been established.
